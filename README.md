# SiF8 Brand Vision

Build a premium, high-end, dark-mode React landing page for a boutique strategy-led brand studio named "SiF8".

Brand Design System & Typography

Colors: Deep void background (#08050f). Core accents: Deep Purple (#231342), Royal Blue (#3125AD), Sky Cyan (#4CB7E4), Violet (#9550B3), and Bright Cyan (#0EFFFF).

Typography: 'Garamond' (serif) for display headlines; 'DM Sans' (sans-serif) for body, subheads, and labels.

Styling: Minimal glassmorphism, 1px subtle borders (rgba(255,255,255,0.1)), ambient radial background glows.

Section Architecture (Render in exact order)

Navbar: Fixed top navigation featuring the logo "SiF8" Links: About, Services, Case Studies, Process, Testimonials, and a "Free Evaluation" button.

Hero Section:

Eyebrow: "Strategy-Led Brand Systems for Muslim Founders"

Headline: "Your work is worth more than your brand is charging for it."

Subheadline: "SiF8 builds brand systems from the inside out — positioning, identity, and voice — so your business becomes legible, trustworthy, and premium. Without deception. Without compromise."

CTA Button: "Apply for Basirah Brand Audit →"

Trust Signal (beneath CTA): "✦ Trusted by 20+ brands"

The Problem Section (The Reality):

Layout & Styling: Classy, minimalist, and editorial. Use generous whitespace and a clean 2-column text grid beneath a large, spanning headline. Separate the two columns with a subtle, 1px vertical dividing line (rgba(255,255,255,0.1)). Set the body text line-height to 1.8 for a breathable, high-end feel.

Headline: "You've built something real. Your market can't see it."

Left Column (The Disconnect): "Your brand doesn't carry the weight of your actual expertise. You over-explain pricing and lose deals to competitors with better optics but inferior delivery."

Right Column (The Fix): "Generic templates and manipulative marketing tactics aren't the answer. You need a brand system that reflects your true values without crossing ethical lines. That is what we fix."

Who This Is For:

Headline: "Built for founders who refuse to trade their deen for their growth."

List (1-5): 01. You're generating revenue but can't charge what your work is worth. 02. You keep having to explain yourself in discovery calls. 03. You got a logo with no strategic backbone behind it. 04. You're quietly anxious that your marketing tactics might be crossing a line. 05. You want to grow without compromising your deen.

Not For Block: "Founders chasing shortcuts, fast logos, or trend-chasing aesthetics... If that's not where you are, we'll tell you honestly — before you spend a single taka."

Why Choose Us: 6-card CSS grid. Central circular, glowing "SiF8" logo orb with a "breathing/pumping" animation. Curved, glowing SVG connector lines linking all 6 cards to this central orb.

The Bridge (Neon Heartbeat): Centered text: "Business and deen were never separate". Glowing, animated SVG neon heartbeat line (EKG style) visually connecting the words 'Business' and 'deen' utilizing CSS stroke-dasharray.
